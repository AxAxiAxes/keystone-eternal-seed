AXI Workspace Library

Private, admin-gated index of everything filed with AXI: apps/services, documents, automations, and the public chat history — one indexed place to browse instead of separate pages. Read-only view; it does not create, approve, or delete anything.

Apps & services

Every service registered in the private service registry, with its current stage.

Documents

Every source filed in the approval-gated source catalog (title, type, classification). Filing a new document still requires the existing source.catalog approval workflow.

Automations

Automation profiles and tracked tasks. Activating, pausing, or creating automations is not done here — see the Automation console.

Chat history, indexed by day

The same public AXIOM chat history shown on /axiom, indexed here day-by-day for quick browsing.
