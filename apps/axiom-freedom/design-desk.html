Questions to bring to your own professional

  • Which parts of this idea affect structure, load, egress, or code compliance, and who is licensed to confirm that in my jurisdiction?
  • What permits, inspections, or approvals does a project like this typically require here?
  • What existing conditions (age, systems, materials) change the scope or cost of this direction?
  • What is a realistic phased timeline, and what would change it?
  • What ongoing maintenance or lifecycle cost should I plan for with these materials?

Cubic card module

Reusable, reorderable, and build-free

Title + glyph Metric / content Status chips Footer action

Each cubic card is a self-contained panel with consistent padding, border, accent strip, circular glyph slot, optional chip row, and optional footer. Reorganize a grid without JavaScript by changing --card-order and --card-span on the card itself.
