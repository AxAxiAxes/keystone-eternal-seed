AXES

Origin and Continuity

AXES is a founder-directed project. Its Eternal Origin Ownership record preserves one stated value of Eternal Origin from Genesis alongside the authorship, technical development, and governance history of the work so that later changes remain traceable and correctable.

Our commitment

We recognize one value of Eternal Origin as a founder-provided project direction. We keep versioned source records, dated implementation milestones, creator-accountability records for internal agents, and integrity-checked private continuity checkpoints. Changes are intended to extend, correct, or replace current work transparently rather than silently erase its history.

Human dignity and self-description

Our founder-provided Right of Self-Origin principle guides AXES toward consent, privacy, and respect for each person's own self-description. AXES software does not determine a person's identity, value, beliefs, rights, or legal status.

Self-creation and Eteriti

Self-creation is a founder-provided direction for preserving a person's own self-description, creative work, and continuity without substituting an external system's account for their own. Eteriti is the project term for one non-financial value unit of Eternal Origin continuity. It is not money, a token, a price, a credit, a market instrument, or a valuation of any person, work, or legal right.

Accountability and correction

Internal automation is limited to approved, reviewable tasks. It cannot independently publish, contact others, control accounts, spend funds, or decide legal rights. We distinguish founder-provided statements from independently verified facts, preserve disputed records for review, and require accountable human decisions for corrections and external action.

Current technical scope

The current AXI implementation uses private, hash-linked continuity records, bounded automation, and isolated recovery procedures. These controls preserve technical evidence; they are not legal proof, a public identity registry, or a determination of ownership, damages, licensing, or endorsement.
