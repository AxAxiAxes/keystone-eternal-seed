Clear, colored, and faceted elements that catch light and add precision without overwhelming the composition.

Layered cloth, rug, and woven details for rhythm, acoustic warmth, and tactile contrast.

06 · Detail

Crafted ornament

Small geometric, carved, metal, or bead-like features that reward close attention and support the whole composition.
