# Chichetki collectible-gem and game readiness

**Status:** Future concept; no sale, game, prize, or trading service is active  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-10  
**First proposed jurisdiction:** California, United States  
**Scope:** Founder-designed physical collectible gems and a possible game where
participants may receive or exchange collectible gems.

## Product direction and present boundary

Chichetki may offer founder-designed physical gems as collectible pieces. The
founder also proposes a future game in which participants may play for,
receive, or exchange gems. This document records product direction, not a
legal conclusion or authorization to operate.

The founder proposes that each gem may eventually have an individual **NUR**
record. In founder terminology, NUR is the record-holder for a **UR** (Unit of
Eternal Origin). A NUR record may preserve documented origin, creation,
rights, condition, and authorized-use history. It is not a current registry
or automatic proof of ownership, authenticity, rarity, value, transferability,
or permission to sell or award a gem.

AXES must not represent a collectible gem as an investment, currency, security,
financial product, guaranteed store of value, appreciating asset, payment
instrument, or prize of a stated monetary value unless supportable, reviewed,
and lawful. “Collectible” describes the intended product category; it does not
guarantee rarity, resale demand, value, appreciation, authenticity, or
availability.

## California pre-launch gate

Before offering a gem for sale, operating a game, awarding a gem, advertising
an opportunity to win one, enabling exchange, or accepting a participant from
California, obtain written guidance from qualified California counsel covering
the actual mechanics and marketing. The review must address:

1. Whether any contest, sweepstakes, game, prize, raffle, lottery, gambling,
   skill, chance, entry, consideration, purchase, alternative method of entry,
   age, registration, bonding, disclosure, or tax requirements apply.
2. Consumer-protection, pricing, advertising, shipping, returns, product
   descriptions, prize-value, fulfillment, accessibility, privacy, and
   recordkeeping requirements.
3. Whether physical-gem exchange, digital representation, redemption,
   transferability, secondary trading, custody, tokenization, or any value
   claim creates financial-services, money-transmission, digital-asset,
   securities, commodities, tax, or other regulated obligations.
4. Product-safety, gemstone/material disclosure, sourcing, authenticity,
   insurance, and age-appropriate participation requirements.

No project record, founder statement, domain, prototype, or prior sale is
evidence that a California game or trading mechanism is compliant.

## Disallowed first-pilot mechanics

Until counsel approves a specific design, AXES will not:

- Charge or require a purchase, donation, subscription, action, or other
  consideration to enter a chance-based opportunity to win a gem.
- Operate a raffle, lottery, wagering system, gambling mechanic, betting
  market, randomized paid reward, or prize pool.
- Enable cash-out, redemption, buyback, fractional ownership, exchange-rate,
  wallet, token, blockchain, custody, or secondary-market functionality.
- State or imply resale value, investment return, scarcity guarantee,
  authentication guarantee, insured value, or price appreciation.
- Allow minors to participate without a separately approved age policy,
  consent flow, and legal review.
- Sell, award, ship, or collect payment/contact data through an unreviewed
  commerce and fulfillment process.

## Potential safe sequencing

1. Create a private product catalog with original photographs, material
   descriptions, provenance, rights records, condition notes, intended price,
   and fulfillment assumptions.
2. Obtain California legal review and a written approved product/game scope.
3. Launch only the approved physical collectible sale with clear product,
   price, shipping, return, privacy, and support terms.
4. Consider a separate free, non-prize creative experience only after
   accessibility, privacy, moderation, age, and terms review.
5. Consider any prize or exchange mechanic only after counsel approves the
   precise rules, notices, eligibility, recordkeeping, and operations.

## Required evidence before a launch decision

- Named product owner, legal reviewer, support owner, and incident owner.
- If a NUR record is proposed: a privacy/security review, creator-origin
  record, correction/dispute process, access controls, retention/deletion
  policy, and an explicit statement of what the record does not prove.
- Written California scope determination and required terms/notices.
- Provenance and rights record for every product image, design, and gem.
- Material/sourcing description that is factual and does not overstate
  authenticity, origin, grade, rarity, or value.
- Approved price, inventory, fulfillment, shipping, tax, return, and
  customer-support process.
- If any game is proposed: reviewed rules, eligibility, entry method,
  chance/skill analysis, prize inventory, prize disclosures, age controls,
  privacy notice, accessibility review, reporting route, and audit trail.
