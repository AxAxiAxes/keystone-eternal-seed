**Status:** Internal candidate-offer sheet; not approved for publication,
solicitation, pricing, or paid delivery
**Recorded:** 2026-09-11
**Accountable owner:** Founder approval required before activation
**Purpose:** Define a bounded creator-origin recordkeeping candidate so the
founder can determine whether a small, private service pilot is ready.

## Candidate offer

**Working identifier:** `KEYSTONE-COS-001`
**Audience:** Creators or small project teams who want help organizing their
own source materials, contributor records, versions, and questions for their
chosen qualified advisor.

**Supported outcome:** A private, client-controlled organization packet that
indexes client-provided materials and identifies factual record gaps. It may
help the client prepare questions and documents for their attorney or other
qualified advisor; it does not answer those questions.

**Candidate deliverables:**

1. A client-provided asset and source inventory with an internal identifier,
   supplied date, version reference, and private storage reference.
2. A provenance index that distinguishes founder-provided, contributor-
   confirmed, licensed/permission-based, collaborative, technical-assessment,
   unverified, and contested source classifications.
3. A version and contribution timeline based solely on materials the client
   supplies or specifically authorizes for review.
4. An evidence-packet index listing provided records, known gaps, preservation
   notes, and questions for the client's chosen qualified advisor.
5. One bounded factual correction pass that updates the packet from
   client-supplied corrections without altering original source records.

**Candidate delivery boundary:** Deliver the packet privately through an
approved founder-controlled channel. The client retains control of their
source materials and decides whether to share the packet with an advisor.

## Explicit exclusions

KEYSTONE must not present or provide this candidate offer as:

- legal advice, legal research, a legal opinion, patent prosecution, trademark
  clearance, copyright registration, filing service, legal representation, or
  dispute resolution;
- a determination of authorship, ownership, inventorship, infringement,
  validity, priority, enforceability, damages, valuation, recovery, or remedy;
- a promise that preservation, hashing, organization, or a registry record
  proves a real-world fact or creates a legal right;
- an investigation of third parties, accusation, takedown request, demand,
  enforcement action, or contact with an external party on the client's behalf;
- a public registry, identity service, credential, asset sale, token,
  marketplace, custody, payment, currency, investment, or financial service;
  or
- a substitute for confidential advice from a qualified professional.

The client remains responsible for their own rights decisions, record
retention, advisor selection, and all legal, financial, business, or external
actions.
