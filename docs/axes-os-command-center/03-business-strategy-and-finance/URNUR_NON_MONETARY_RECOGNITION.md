**Scope:** Voluntary recognition within the AXES Ecosystem of the Soul
**Owner:** Founder or a named human delegate

## Purpose

URNUR may acknowledge constructive, consented contributions to AXES creative,
learning, stewardship, and community work. Recognition exists to make
contribution visible and creditable; it is not money, compensation, a benefit,
a credential, a reputation score, or a measure of a person's worth.

The phrases **recognition energy**, **resequencer**, **transmission**, and
**alignment** may be used only as voluntary creative and reflective language
for an experience, ritual, artwork, editorial practice, or shared project
process. They do not describe a technical, medical, psychological,
scientific, diagnostic, therapeutic, or influence system.

## Constitutional boundaries

URNUR recognition must be:

1. **Non-monetary and non-transferable:** It has no cash value, price,
   redemption, sale, exchange, payment, wage, investment, token, wallet,
   market, dividend, or financial-return function.
2. **Voluntary and consented:** A participant may decline recognition, choose
   the approved form of credit, request correction, or request removal review.
3. **Contribution-based:** Acknowledgements describe a specific, documented
   contribution or stewardship action; they do not rank personal identity,
   belief, emotion, health, talent, loyalty, or social standing.
4. **Human-reviewed:** A named human reviews the contribution record and
   approves public credit. No automated system may assign recognition,
   calculate a level, infer alignment, or decide eligibility.
5. **Private by default:** Evidence and participant records remain private.
   Public recognition needs the participant's documented permission for the
   exact wording and context.
6. **Revisable:** Every acknowledgement has a correction/removal path,
   version history, and human escalation owner.

## Prohibited recognition mechanics

AXES must not implement or claim:

- Energy levels, aura readings, frequency readings, alignment scores,
  spiritual status, soul status, personal rankings, or resequencing outcomes.
- Monitoring, profiling, biometric collection, behavioral scoring, automated
  inference, surveillance, or algorithmic eligibility.
- A promise to transmit, receive, alter, harmonize, heal, protect, diagnose,
  or otherwise affect a person's physical, emotional, spiritual, financial,
  or social condition.
- Financial incentives, monetary conversion, tradable units, tokenization,
  payment access, discounts conditioned on recognition, or sale of recognition
  records.
- Public disclosure of a participant's identity, contribution, affiliation,
  or personal information without documented permission.

## Allowed initial forms

The first pilot, if approved, may use one or more of these private or
permissioned acknowledgements:

| Form | Meaning | Required record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contribution credit | Names or pseudonymizes a defined creative, learning, or stewardship contribution | Contributor preference, contribution description, source/evidence, reviewer, approved wording |
| Project provenance entry | Connects approved work to its documented origin record | Origin record ID, rights/consent status, reviewer, version history |
| Reflection acknowledgment | Allows a participant to mark a voluntary personal reflection or completed practice | Participant-controlled wording, privacy setting, no evaluation or score |
| Stewardship acknowledgment | Recognizes a documented act that helped maintain an approved project or community space | Scope, date, human reviewer, permission for any public mention |

No form is automatic, cumulative, competitive, or redeemable. AXES must avoid
levels, badges with implied status, leaderboards, streaks, ranking language,
or any interface that pressures participation.

## Record and review process

Before recording recognition, the human owner must confirm:

1. The contribution is specific, accurate, and appropriate to acknowledge.
2. The participant has chosen the permitted credit and privacy treatment.
3. The record follows `AXES_CREATOR_ORIGIN_CONSTITUTION.md` and contains no
   unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
4. The wording does not imply an unverified origin, ownership, personal
   quality, health result, spiritual fact, endorsement, or financial value.
5. A correction/removal route and a named response owner exist.

## Readiness gate

No public URNUR experience may launch until the project has a reviewed pilot
brief, participant notice, consent and removal flow, creator-origin record
model, privacy/security review, named human owner, accessibility review, and
complaint/escalation route. Any proposed financial, token, market, payment, or
investment feature remains blocked by `URNUR_FINANCIAL_READINESS.md` and
requires separate written legal, compliance, security, and operational
approval.
