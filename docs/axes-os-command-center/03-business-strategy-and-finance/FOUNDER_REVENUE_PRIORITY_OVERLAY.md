# Founder revenue-priority overlay

**Status:** Internal sequencing overlay; does not replace KEYSTONE V1-V8  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-10  
**Purpose:** Focus limited early budget on lawful, supportable client value while
the broader KEYSTONE architecture continues to be built.

## Founder priority

The founder directs parallel development of three revenue-oriented tracks:

| Priority track | Product direction | Immediate purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Trading application | Preserve and develop the product architecture without live financial activity |
| V2 | AXES appraisals and inspections | Prioritize client-facing property, design, condition, and project-discovery pathways |
| V3 | KEYSTONE | Build the creator-origin, governance, evidence, and operational foundation |

This overlay does not rename, replace, or modify the original V1-V8 venture
sequence preserved in the patent source. It identifies which work receives
near-term execution attention under a constrained budget.

## Revenue-first sequence

1. **V2 AXES:** Start with services that can lawfully be delivered now:
   design/materials consultation, project documentation, visual concept work,
   condition-observation intake, and referrals to qualified professionals.
   These may produce the earliest client revenue.
2. **V3 KEYSTONE:** Offer carefully scoped creator-origin record setup,
   private asset inventory, provenance organization, and lawyer-ready evidence
   packet preparation. This is operational recordkeeping, not legal advice or
   legal enforcement.
3. **V1 Trading application:** Continue internal architecture, research,
   synthetic-data prototyping, and counsel preparation. Do not use it to
   generate revenue or provide financial functionality until the written
   permitted scope is approved.

## Appraisal and inspection boundary

AXES must not advertise or deliver a property appraisal, home inspection,
engineering inspection, code inspection, valuation, licensed architecture,
brokerage, safety certification, or regulated professional opinion unless the
actual jurisdiction, licensed responsible professional, insurance, scope,
terms, and required consumer disclosures are in place.

Until then, AXES may provide non-professional design consultation, general
project documentation, visual/material direction, a client-controlled project
questionnaire, and referrals. All public language must distinguish those
services from an appraisal, inspection, valuation, or certification.

## Budget discipline

- Use existing public infrastructure and founder-created materials first.
- Require a clear scope, price, owner, delivery process, and customer-support
  path before accepting paid work.
- Do not spend on trading, currency, banking, collectible-gem games,
  marketplaces, paid advertising, or external automation before the associated
  legal, security, and operational gate is satisfied.
- Reinvest early revenue in email continuity, backup/recovery, source
  preservation, rights records, portfolio quality, and qualified review.
