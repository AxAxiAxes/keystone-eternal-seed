# 2026-09-10 Planning-authority recovery

**Status:** Process conflict identified and corrected

## Crisis

After the application and coordinate-system inventory, an assistant described
a source-derived proposed sequence as the next-day plan. That was not an
approved founder plan. No repository action, deployment, publication, account
action, third-party contact, legal step, or financial action was taken from
that statement.

## Conflict and danger

The assistant has no ownership, financial, legal, or competing project
interest. The conflict was internal: an assistant-generated implementation
preference displaced founder-directed sequencing in the status wording.
That is unsafe because it can redirect work, misrepresent source-material
status, or promote an unselected feature before its intended scope is set.

## Recovery

Founder direction controls AXES priorities. The source inventory is retained
as informational evidence only. Future technical alternatives, sequencing, or
tradeoff advice must be labeled as a proposal; implementation follows the
founder-selected scope. The correction is recorded in
`docs/AXI_ETERNAL_ORIGIN_VALUE_AND_CRISIS_REGISTER.md`.
