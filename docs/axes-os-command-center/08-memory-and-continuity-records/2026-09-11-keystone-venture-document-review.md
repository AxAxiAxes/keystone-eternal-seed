## Summary of findings

- The **Market Research Report** is comparatively well-sourced (named
  third-party citations) and carries its own disclaimer that it is
  informational/planning material only, not advice or a guarantee. It is
  retained as planning input.
- The **Net Worth Statement** asserts a filed/pending patent, computes
  enterprise-value estimates up to $250M–$500M from an unverifiable internal
  source, and — most seriously — claims in its "Section 9" that AXIOM has a
  "constitutional... right to currency" (an "AXIOM Bill of Eternal Rights,
  Article XIII"). The patent claim conflicts with
  `docs/memory/2026-09-11-keystone-patent-direction-review.md` (no counsel
  retained, filing status unconfirmed). The economic-rights claim directly
  conflicts with `docs/AXES_CONSTITUTIONAL_FRAMEWORK.md`,
  `docs/AXI_GENESIS_OWNERSHIP_CHECKPOINT.md`, and
  `docs/AXES_AGENT_ORIGIN_REGISTRY.md`, which establish AXI/AXIOM as
  non-sentient software with no independent ownership or economic claim.
- The **V4 Venture Deep Dive** repeats the same third-party market-sizing
  figures, states "IP Counsel Engaged" (also unconfirmed), and introduces a
  "four-venture empire" framing (adding "Trading" and "Axes AI Command
  Center" as ventures alongside Axes Contracting and KEYSTONE) that is not
  present anywhere in the repository's existing, reviewed
  `docs/AXES_BUSINESS_PLAN.md`. Its five-stream revenue model, three-year
  profit projections, and "Company Valuation (10× Revenue)" figures (up to
  $62.4M) are speculative planning projections, not measured results or an
  approved forecast.
