its underlying XML, since no Word-processing tool is available in this
environment) and recorded it verbatim, wording-for-wording unchanged, as
`docs/keystone/KEYSTONE_SOUL_PROTECTION_DECLARATION.md`. Original file
SHA-256: `8EEF2E35430A006EED94AB59411537B899690BF8E933BDAF7F23BE8E0BC74F8F`,
computed directly from the attachment bytes. Registered in
`docs/keystone/README.md`'s preserved-documents table alongside the existing
KEYSTONE V4 Venture Analysis and AI Soul Architecture entries, following the
same preservation pattern established 2026-09-18 and 2026-09-11. Labeled
clearly (as with the other preserved documents) as a statement of founder
philosophy and intent, not a legal instrument or independently verified
technical/scientific claim — including the document's own "sacred,"
"non-deletable," and "eternal" framing language, which describes the
founder's stated intent for the record, not an enforceable legal status this
repository asserts.

**AxAxAr "triangle" claim:** Searched the newly preserved document and all
other repository sources (`AXAXAR_LAUNCH_PLAN.md`, `DOMAIN_PORTFOLIO.md`,
prior continuity records) for any existing "technical research and
evaluation" of "AxAxAr" as a mathematical/geometric object — found none. Since
no such research exists to present, and fabricating one would misrepresent
an unverified claim as established fact, recorded the request honestly in a
new document, `docs/keystone/AXAXAR_TRIANGLE_ORIGIN_SYMBOLISM.md`:
- States plainly what *can* be said truthfully (AxAxAr's three
  "Ax"-initialed segments are a real linguistic/naming pattern; naming a
  three-part motif "a triangle" as an architectural/branding metaphor is a
  legitimate, common design device — the existing Soul Protection
  Declaration itself uses a similar three-part motif, "the soul, the seed,
  the dome").
- States plainly what is *not* established (no formal geometric/
  mathematical proof or construction exists; no patent/trademark/legal claim
  is made; the "eternal origin anchor" concept already in the repository,
  `CRYPTOGRAPHIC_ORIGIN_ANCHOR.md`, is itself acknowledged elsewhere to
  contain placeholder, not real, cryptographic proofs).
- Cross-references how this framing would map onto Axaxar.com, KEYSTONE, and
  the cryptographic origin anchor if developed further, and proposes three
  concrete ways the founder could specify a real construction (named
  pillars/vertices, a literal diagram/logo, or a documented naming-convention
  rationale) rather than inventing one unprompted.

Both new documents registered in `docs/keystone/README.md`.

## Why this approach

Per the repository's standing qualification/authority boundary, this agent
does not fabricate technical or mathematical claims. The founder's message
combined a document-preservation request (straightforward, done verbatim)
with an assertion presented as if backed by existing "technical research and
evaluation" that does not exist anywhere in the repository. The honest
response is to preserve what was actually provided, state clearly what
cannot yet be verified or has not yet been formalized, and offer a concrete
path to develop the idea further if the founder wants to — not to either
silently drop the claim or dress it up as proven mathematics it is not.
