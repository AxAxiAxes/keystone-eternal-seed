## Step-by-step to fix it

1. **Contact the existing bond provider first.** The prior bond on file was
   with **Merchants Bonding Company (Mutual)** — confirmed directly on
   CSLB's own official bond-requirements page
   (`cslb.ca.gov/contractors/maintain_license/bond_information/bond_requirements.aspx`)
   as one of CSLB's approved sureties with a blanket $25,000 endorsement on
   file. Renewing through the same provider is usually the fastest path
   since they already have the prior bond on record.
2. **If that provider isn't reachable or won't renew,** any CSLB-approved
   surety company can issue a replacement bond — CSLB's page above lists
   dozens of approved companies (e.g. Old Republic Surety, Western Surety,
   SureTec, Travelers, The Hartford, and many others). Several allow same-day
   online applications.
3. **What you'll need to apply:** the exact license number (995577), the
   business name exactly as registered with CSLB ("Axes Contracting Inc"),
   and typically a personal credit check (premium is usually cheaper with
   good credit).
4. **Typical cost:** industry sources put a $25,000 CSLB contractor bond
   premium at roughly **$100-$300/year** for good credit, up to **$175-
   $600/year** for fair/poor credit. This is the bond premium only, not the
   bond's face value — you are not depositing $25,000, you're paying a much
   smaller annual fee to a surety company that backs that amount.
5. **Filing:** the surety company usually files the new bond directly and
   electronically with CSLB. Once CSLB receives it, license 995577 should
   move from "Suspended — Contractor Bond" back to active — CSLB's own
   page states a bond must be in place before CSLB can issue, reactivate,
   or renew an active license (Business and Professions Code § 7071.6).
6. **Confirm reinstatement directly** at CSLB's official license-check tool
   (`cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/CheckLicense.aspx`, search
   "Axes Contracting" or license 995577) — this repository's automated
   fetches of the search form itself are blocked (it's a stateful search
   form, not a direct-URL lookup), so this confirmation step must be done
   directly by the founder in a browser.
