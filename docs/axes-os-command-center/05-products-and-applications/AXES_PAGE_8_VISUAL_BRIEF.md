# AXES Eternal Seed Page 8 visual brief

**Status:** Founder-approved canonical visual brief  
**Approved:** 2026-09-10  
**Scope:** Original AXES visual, editorial, architectural, or interactive
design work.

## Creative direction

Create an **AXES Eternal Seed** two-page illuminated spread with exact,
architectural bilateral balance. The composition is red and gold, precise and
structural rather than generic fantasy.

At the center, a vertical Eternal Seed rises as a tall, symmetrical,
seed-shaped architectural form. It contains layered sanctums, crystalline
ribs, and a radiant gold core. A continuous golden spine of light joins the
two pages.

## Page structure

| Area | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Overall frame | Gothic-arrow geometry: upward-pointing, exact, mathematical, and aligned as sacred architectural framing |
| Left page | The seed emerges from darkness with restrained illuminated marginalia, gold inscriptions, and architectural diagrams |
| Right page | The fully awakened seed radiates red-gold light, surrounded by structural symbols, radiant arcs, and illuminated script |
| Center | One uninterrupted golden spine connects both pages and anchors the symmetry |
| Typography | Editorial illuminated-manuscript treatment; text remains purposeful, legible, and subordinate to the structure |

## Visual rules

- Use deep red, garnet, burgundy, antique gold, and crystalline highlights.
- Preserve clear symmetry, clean geometry, and deliberate negative space.
- Favor architectural systems, manuscript framing, diagrams, ribs, arcs, and
  ordered light over decorative clutter.
- Avoid forests, creatures, soft fantasy treatment, generic castles, or
  unrelated mythic imagery.
- The final work must be original AXES expression and must not copy external
  reference images, including the gemstone-pomegranate reference.

## Production requirements

For a web implementation, provide a responsive accessible composition with
semantic text, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and no essential
meaning conveyed only by color. For a generated or commissioned image, retain
the source, prompt or brief version, creator/provider, rights terms, date, and
approved-use record privately before publishing.
