# Temple of Love — Architecture Page (Project Eternal Seed)

**Status:** Design concept, distilled from founder-provided material. Not a
built structure, not an engineering plan, and not reviewed by a licensed
architect or structural engineer. This document records a creative
architectural vision for KEYSTONE / Project Eternal Seed; any real-world
construction would require independent professional design, permitting, and
structural review.

## Origin of this record

Founder Axel Urartu developed this design across a personal AI chat session
(part of a stated "32-page Project Eternal Seed manuscript," continuing from
an earlier "Page 7 — AXES Keystone Logo Page"). The founder shared the raw
chat transcript with the instruction to treat it as meaningful creative
material to be "sprouted, merged, deconstructed and reconstructed" rather than
discarded. That raw transcript is **not** committed here (it is a personal
chat export, largely repeated re-deliveries of the same content due to a
chat-rendering issue on the founder's side); this document distills the final,
stable version of the design content itself, which is the part with lasting
project value.

## Page 8 — Temple of Love (two-page architectural spread)

The AXES Keystone Logo (established on Page 7) crowns the top center of both
pages of this spread, acting as the structural/title anchor.

### Left page — Temple of Love: Technical Blueprint

A traced architectural blueprint built from the following elements:

- Pomegranate-seed diagonal grid
- Golden measurement arcs
- Structural alignment lines
- Chess-rug zones
- Mosaic diagonal band
- Modular wall tracks
- Fixed kitchen
- Fixed spa
- Apex pomegranate waterfall
- Front court and well
- Dome entry hall
- Four tall chimney-columns
- Balcony deck
- Flower cage and pond
- Exterior waterfall connection

### Right page — Temple of Love: 3D Massing, Interior, Ecology, Meaning

**3D massing (Palacio form):** central dome, four pillars, balcony deck,
chimney-columns, modular interior volumes, exterior terrain, waterfall
feeding the spa, garden and pond, front court and gate axis.

**Interior grid (pomegranate altar geometry):** red-gold modular grid,
chess-rug patterning, mosaic diagonals, seed-geometry floor plan, light-path
corridors, sacred proportion zones.

**Ecology system:** waterfall → spa → pond → well cycle; fireplaces in the
four pillars; air circulation through the chimney-columns; natural light
entering the dome; flower-cage ecology; regeneration garden; water and plant
interaction.
