# AXES design and materials consultation — intake template (draft)

**Status:** Internal draft template only; not published, not distributed to
any client, and not usable for paid delivery until `AXES-DMC-001` is founder
approved per `AXES_DESIGN_MATERIALS_CONSULTATION_READINESS.md`
**Recorded:** 2026-09-13
**Purpose:** Provide the structured, client-controlled intake form referenced
by that readiness sheet's first candidate deliverable, so activation is not
blocked on drafting one from scratch once the founder approves the offer.

## Scope and boundary

This template only organizes information a client would voluntarily provide
about their own project. It:

- collects no payment, banking, identity-document, insurance, medical, or
  government-ID information;
- collects no property address, parcel/legal-description, or other
  location-identifying detail beyond what the client chooses to share for
  aesthetic context;
- makes no promise of price, turnaround, outcome, licensing scope, or
  professional opinion; and
- is not itself an offer, solicitation, contract, or public-facing form. It
  is a private internal artifact until the founder approves both the
  `AXES-DMC-001` offer and a specific delivery/collection channel for it.

Every completed copy of this template must carry the same non-professional
service boundary already defined in
`AXES_DESIGN_MATERIALS_CONSULTATION_READINESS.md` and
`FOUNDER_REVENUE_PRIORITY_OVERLAY.md`.

## Template

**Client-facing disclaimer (must accompany every use):**

> This questionnaire supports a non-professional visual/material direction
> consultation only. It is not an architecture, engineering, code-compliance,
> safety, appraisal, or inspection service, and it does not substitute for
> advice from a licensed professional. Please do not include payment,
> financial, medical, or government-identification information in your
> answers.

1. **Project objective** — In your own words, what are you hoping this space
   or project will feel or look like when finished?
2. **Project type** — General category only (e.g., single room, exterior,
   small addition, landscape). Not a scope-of-work or licensed-service
   request.
3. **Inspiration references** — Links, descriptions, or attached images the
   client already has permission to share (their own photos, or sources they
   confirm are appropriately licensed/public). AXES does not source or
   verify third-party rights on the client's behalf.
4. **Palette and material preferences** — Preferred colors, textures,
   finishes, or materials, and any the client wants to avoid.
5. **Constraints the client wants considered** — Budget range (optional,
   descriptive only, not a price quote request), timeline hopes, or existing
   elements to keep. No cost, availability, or purchasing advice is provided
   in return.
6. **Known regulatory/professional involvement** — Has the client already
   engaged, or plan to engage, a licensed architect, engineer, contractor,
   inspector, or appraiser? (Informational only; AXES does not verify
   licenses or make referral guarantees.)
7. **Preferred output** — Which of the candidate deliverables in
   `AXES_DESIGN_MATERIALS_CONSULTATION_READINESS.md` is the client most
   interested in (direction brief, material/palette board, discussion
   questions for their own providers)?
8. **Confidentiality acknowledgment** — Client-checked confirmation that they
   understand the private-delivery boundary and have not included payment,
   financial, medical, or identity information above.

## Activation dependency

This template does not authorize collection, storage, or use of any client
information. Before any real client's answers are collected, the founder
must still complete every open item in
`AXES_DESIGN_MATERIALS_CONSULTATION_READINESS.md`'s activation record
(scope, owner, price basis, terms, capacity, rights review, delivery
channel, and Tier 1 evidence-ledger assumptions). Completed client answers
must be stored outside this repository in an approved private system, per
that readiness sheet's evidence practice.
