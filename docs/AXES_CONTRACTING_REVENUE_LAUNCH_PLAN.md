## Important distinction the founder must resolve before any public claim

The CSLB license held (Class B, General Building Contractor) authorizes
**general contracting/construction work**, not necessarily a formal "home
inspector certification." The archived site's language ("certified home
inspectors... reports accepted by industry standards... for homeowners,
banks, insurance companies") may describe a different credential/scope
than the current B-license alone confers. **Do not re-publish the
archived "certified home inspectors" language verbatim** until the
founder confirms:

1. Whether the historical home-inspection work was performed under this
   same CSLB license, a separate certification (e.g. ASHI/InterNACHI home
   inspector certification), or a subcontracted inspector network.
2. Whether that separate certification (if any) is still current.
3. Whether general contracting work (repairs, remediation, construction)
   can be advertised and performed today under the current B-license
   alone, independent of any inspection-specific claim.

This matters because `FOUNDER_REVENUE_PRIORITY_OVERLAY.md`'s existing
boundary rule is explicit: AXES must not advertise "home inspection...
unless the actual jurisdiction, licensed responsible professional,
insurance, scope, terms, and required consumer disclosures are in place."
That rule was written when the license status looked suspended; it still
applies now that the license is active — active-and-current is necessary
but may not by itself be sufficient for every historical claim.

## Proposed phased launch (draft — needs founder sign-off at each phase)

### Phase A — general contracting/remediation services (lower risk, ready sooner)
Advertise and accept work squarely within what a Class B general
contractor license covers without requiring a separate inspection
certification: general repairs, remediation/reconstruction after
fire/water/wind damage, structural repair, and project-management
services. This is the safer starting scope because it maps directly to
the license actually confirmed active, with no unresolved certification
question.

### Phase B — home inspection services (only after the certification question above is resolved)
Re-introduce inspection-specific service language only once the founder
confirms the certification basis above. If a separate inspector
certification has lapsed, either renew it, formally partner/subcontract
with a currently-certified inspector, or drop inspection-specific
marketing language and keep the offering to Phase A's contracting/
remediation scope.

### What's needed to go live on Phase A (founder decisions, not code)
- Confirm current insurance/liability coverage (workers' comp is noted
  "exempt" per the CSLB record — confirm this still reflects reality if
  any employees or subcontractors will be used).
- Confirm service area (the archived site did not specify a service
  radius from the Glendale, CA address).
- Approve a pricing/estimate approach (even a simple "free estimate,
  scope-dependent quote" model needs an explicit yes).
- Decide the client-intake channel: phone (424-334-0777, per the legacy
  record), a contact form, or both.
- Resolve `FOUNDER_ACTION_QUEUE.md` item #3 (axescontracting.com DNS/HTTPS
  cert renewal) — the domain must be reachable and trusted before it's
  used as the public-facing entry point.

### What I can prepare now, without needing new founder authority

  no inspection claims) for founder review before any page goes live.
