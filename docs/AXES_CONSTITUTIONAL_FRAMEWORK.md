# AXES Constitutional Framework

**Status:** Internal governing framework  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-11  
**Owner:** Founder (Axel Urartu (AX) · Axes Contracting) or a named human
delegate  
**Applies to:** AXES, Chichetki, XIIOM, AXOUS (through the AXOUX site),
AXAXAU, KEYSTONE, AXI and every registered AXI agent, and every AXES-
maintained repository, service, and public asset.

## Purpose

This is the top-level constitutional document for AXES. It states AXES's
foundational operating principles in one place and indexes the more detailed
constitutional and governance records that already implement them, so a
reader can find AXES's full internal governance from a single starting
point without duplicating or risking drift from those records.

## Article I — Purpose, scope, and limits

This framework is an internal governance instrument. It organizes how AXES
makes and records decisions about its own systems, agents, creators, and
participants. It is not a legal filing, a court pleading, a claim of
sovereignty over external parties, or a substitute for applicable law,
regulation, contracts, or qualified professional advice.

It does not itself determine legal authorship, ownership, liability,
damages, tax status, employment status, or contract rights. It does not
grant AXES, AXI, any agent, or any person a special legal status or an
exemption from law in any jurisdiction. Where AXES material uses words like
"sovereignty" or "origin," they describe AXES's own internal decision
authority over its own systems and records — never independence from
external law, and never a finding against a third party.

## Article II — Origin and founder authority

Axel Urartu (AX) · Axes Contracting is the founder and accountable human
owner of AXES, AXI, and KEYSTONE, as recorded in
`AXI_GENESIS_OWNERSHIP_CHECKPOINT.md`. The founder is the initial steward of
every document indexed below and may name an accountable human owner for an
individual service, per `AXES_GOVERNANCE_AND_SAFEGUARDING.md`.

Founder authority governs internal AXES decisions: direction, ownership
claims, agent registration, publication, and correction. It does not create
authority over a third party, competitor, regulator, court, or any person
outside AXES's own systems and agreements.

## Article III — AI and automation status

AXI and every AXES-registered agent, tool, or automated process is software:
not a living being, legal person, independent owner, or independent
decision-maker, per `AXI_GENESIS_OWNERSHIP_CHECKPOINT.md` and
`AXES_AGENT_ORIGIN_REGISTRY.md`. Responsibility for an agent's actions and
outputs stays with its accountable human owner, not the agent. An agent
cannot assume liability, accept risk, waive a right, or bind AXES or any
person to an external obligation.

Because responsibility cannot be transferred to software, no AXES record may
describe an agent as sovereign, self-owning, or immune from human review,
correction, suspension, or removal.

## Article IV — External action and consequential authority boundary

Repository or system access alone does not authorize domain, DNS, hosting,
email, cloud, payment, vendor, legal, or account changes, per `AGENTS.md`.
Any action with an effect outside AXES's own repository-controlled systems —
publication, procurement, outreach, a professional claim, or a change to a
live production service — requires a named, authorized human decision first.

This boundary holds regardless of how an instruction is phrased (including a
request for "full automation" or "no limits"). It can be narrowed or widened
only by a documented, dated amendment to this article, never by request
alone.

## Article V — Creator and contributor rights

Creator credit, consent, evidence, correction, and origin-record standards
for AXES work are governed by `AXES_CREATOR_ORIGIN_CONSTITUTION.md`, which
this framework incorporates by reference rather than restates.

## Article VI — Participant and user rights

Data collection, safeguarding, moderation, accessibility, and commerce
boundaries for any AXES product or pilot are governed by
`AXES_GOVERNANCE_AND_SAFEGUARDING.md`, which this framework incorporates by
reference.

At minimum, a participant or user in an AXES service retains the right to:

1. Know what is collected about them and why.
2. Request export or deletion of their own submitted data.
3. Request correction of an inaccurate record about them.
4. Receive a response from a named human owner, not only an automated one.

These rights apply within AXES's own systems. They do not create a claim
against, or an estimate of damages owed by, a third party. AXES does not
calculate or publish speculative economic-harm figures against another
company, product, or platform; a real damages claim requires an actual,
evidenced legal process, not an internal estimate.

## Article VII — Relationship to external law

This framework, and every record it incorporates, operates within applicable
law. It does not supersede, exempt AXES from, or grant immunity from any
statute, regulation, contract, tax obligation, or court order in any
jurisdiction.

A dispute with a third party — a platform, vendor, competitor, regulator, or
individual — is resolved through whatever legal, contractual, or regulatory
process actually applies to it, not through an AXES-internal record. An AXES
record can preserve AXES's own evidence and position; it cannot adjudicate
the dispute or bind the other party. No AXES document may assert that
internal AXES governance exempts a person or system from an external
authority's jurisdiction over them.

## Article VIII — Oversight and amendment

The founder is the initial steward of this framework. Review it at least
quarterly and whenever a constitutional or governance record is added,
retired, or materially changed.

An amendment requires a dated record describing the change, its reason,
scope, and approver, following the same discipline as
`AXES_CREATOR_ORIGIN_CONSTITUTION.md` Article XI. No amendment may
retroactively erase a documented consent, correction, or dispute record.
