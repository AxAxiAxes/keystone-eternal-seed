## Hard blocker: youth and age safeguards

Per `AXES_GOVERNANCE_AND_SAFEGUARDING.md`'s youth-safeguards section, none of
the following exist yet: an age-policy/jurisdiction review, guardian
consent/notice workflow, separated adult/minor spaces, a trained human
moderator with an escalation process, or controls against direct adult-minor
messaging, location sharing, financial activity, or sensitive-profile
collection. **The safe default, already on record, is an adult-only first
cohort.** No age-mixed or minor-facing pilot should be attempted before a
youth program is separately designed and approved. This sheet does not
change or advance that default.

## ikigai reflection data sensitivity

Per `AXES_PLATFORM_PLAN.md`, participants must retain control over whether
reflections are stored, shared, or deleted. Because this data (personal
values, interests, strengths) is more sensitive than a typical submission, it
needs its own explicit data map and storage/retention/export/delete mechanism
before any pilot — not just the general platform-wide commitment.
