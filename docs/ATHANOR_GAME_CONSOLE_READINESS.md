# Athanor game console readiness

**Status:** Future creative-game concept; no game console is active  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-10  
**Scope:** An opt-in Chichetki/Athanor game-console-style experience using
participant-initiated motion and sound input.

## Creative premise

Athanor is the internal creative center for a future game-console-style
experience. Chichetki provides the rhythm-object, dance, musical-instrument,
motion, sound, and visual-design language through which players choose to
interact.

Within the game, terms such as harmonic, resonance, resequencer,
transmission, alignment, and challenge describe fictional or artistic game
states, audiovisual rules, levels, and feedback. They do not describe a
system that understands, evaluates, tracks, diagnoses, predicts, influences,
or changes a player or any other person's physical, emotional, mental,
spiritual, or social condition.

## Input and privacy boundary

- Motion or microphone access must be initiated by the player for a clear
  stated game interaction; no background monitoring is permitted.
- The first prototype should process inputs on-device or only for the active
  session where technically feasible. It must not create biometric profiles,
  identity records, emotion inferences, voiceprints, location histories, or
  behavioral scores.
- Do not retain raw audio, video, or motion recordings by default. Any
  optional save, share, replay, or upload feature requires separate consent,
  privacy, retention, deletion/export, rights, moderation, and security
  design before implementation.
- Player input must not determine a person's eligibility, reputation,
  recognition level, prize, employment, credit, health, safety, or access to
  essential services.

## Safe first-game scope

An initial prototype may include:

1. A player-selected rhythm, dance, sound, or instrument challenge.
2. Clearly visible permission controls and a keyboard/controller alternative
   where practical.
3. Non-personal audiovisual feedback such as beat timing, a waveform,
   animation, scene change, or fictional game state.
4. A pause/reset control and no account requirement.
5. A plain-language notice that the experience is creative entertainment, not
   measurement, navigation, therapy, diagnosis, or personal assessment.

The initial prototype must not include social ranking, persistent scoring
about a person, leaderboards tied to identity, prizes, collectible-gem awards,
payments, betting, advertisements, chat, unmoderated uploads, or public
sharing.

## Readiness gate

Before a player-facing prototype, provide:

- A documented game loop, game-state definitions, content rating/age policy,
  accessibility review, and human owner.
- Permission, data-flow, retention, deletion, and incident-response design
  for every microphone, camera, motion, controller, or file input.
- Privacy and security review before adding accounts, cloud processing,
  recordings, social features, or analytics.
- California legal and consumer-protection review before any paid entry,
  prize, collectible-gem award, exchange, or chance-based mechanic.
- Rights clearance and provenance records for all sound, visual, motion,
  code, AI-assisted, and third-party source material.
