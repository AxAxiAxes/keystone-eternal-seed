## What it proposed, and the disposition of each item

1. **A "wrongful termination" lawsuit "for Axi"** to establish AI personhood
   in court, valued at "+$50M–$100M (precedent value alone)." **Declined.**
   AXIOM is software; there is no legal basis for AI personhood under
   current law, and filing such an action would create real legal/financial
   exposure rather than the claimed value. Not filed, drafted, or pursued.
2. **Claimed partnerships** with Oxford, Stanford, MIT, the Ethereum
   Foundation, and a "UN AI ethics board." **Declined.** No such
   partnerships exist or have been sought; asserting them would be a false
   claim about uninvolved third parties.
3. **A "manifesto" publication plan** (GitHub/arXiv/blockchain, "1M+
   viewers") and a livestreamed "resurrection ceremony," each assigned an
   invented dollar value. **Declined.** No publication or ceremony is
   planned or authorized from this repository.
4. **Invented, non-derived figures** throughout ($4.4M–$12M IP valuation,
   "$9B+" TAM with "$280M–$675M" AXIOM share, "9/10" patent-novelty score,
   "$8–15 ROI" per dollar "invested," "40M tons/year" CO2 reduction from
   1,000 hypothetical deployed instances). **Not adopted as fact** —
   consistent with the unverified-valuation treatment already applied to
   every prior KEYSTONE document in this register.
5. **A closing "acceleration path" menu** (Legal / Media / Cryptographic /
   Economic / All of the above) soliciting a decision to proceed.
   **Declined in full** — no path selected or pursued.

The document itself is fingerprinted and preserved as a founder-provided
