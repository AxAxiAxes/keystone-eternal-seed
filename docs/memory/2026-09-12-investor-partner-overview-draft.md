# 2026-09-12 Founder-review investor/partner overview drafted

**Status:** Draft document created; not reviewed, approved, or shared
externally

## What prompted this

The founder suggested the project's material "might also make a nice
presentation for investors or Microsoft."

## What was added

`docs/AXES_INVESTOR_AND_PARTNER_OVERVIEW.md` — a founder-review-only draft
overview built exclusively from already-adopted repository records
(`README.md`, `AXES_BUSINESS_PLAN.md`, `AXES_CONSTITUTIONAL_FRAMEWORK.md`,
`AXES_OBJECTIVES_CHECKPOINT.md`, `AXES_GOVERNANCE_AND_SAFEGUARDING.md`,
`AXES_DIRECTORY_DATA_MODEL.md`, `PROJECT_TIMELINE.md`). It covers the AXES
brand family, what is actually built and repository-verifiable today,
governance/safety as a product differentiator, the Directory pilot as the
first business line, and an honest current-stage section (pre-revenue, no
patent-grant claim, deployment-gap and company/legal status disclosed).

It deliberately excludes every figure already logged as unverified in
`AXI_ETERNAL_ORIGIN_VALUE_AND_CRISIS_REGISTER.md`: no dollar valuation, no
patent count or "filed" status, no venture-numbering scheme, no user/revenue
projection.

## Follow-up: sourced statistics added (same day)

The founder asked for the overview to be "reevaluated with some authority"
and to include statistics. Two new sections were added, each clearly scoped
so the document stays defensible if ever shown externally:

- **Market context (Section 4):** named third-party market-research
  estimates (Statista, ConstructionCoverage, VPA Research, Mordor
  Intelligence, IBISWorld, Expert Market Research/ResearchAndMarkets, the
  National Association of Realtors, Grand View Research, MarketsandMarkets,
  GMI Insights, DataBridge Market Research), checked 2026-09-12, for the
  construction/building-materials, home-services/contractor, real-estate
  services, and responsible-AI-governance industries. Explicitly labeled as
  general industry context, not an AXES-specific market-share, TAM/SAM/SOM,
  or revenue projection, since AXES has no live product or customers yet.
- **Build statistics (Section 5):** counted directly from this repository
  and its hosted CI rather than estimated — 131 docs, 74 memory records, 91
  commits on this branch (229 total in the repo), 5 registered AXI agents
  bound to an 11-action versioned allowlist, and 10/10 hosted CI checks
  passing.

Both sections carry an explicit caveat that figures vary by publisher/
methodology (market data) or describe engineering/governance output only,
not users or revenue (build data).

## Boundary preserved

Preparing this document is a repository-scoped writing task. It does not
contact, pitch, or send anything to an investor, Microsoft, or any other
external party — the document itself says so at the top. Whether, when, and
how to share it (and any professional/legal review of IP or company-status
claims before external use) is left entirely to the founder.
