# AXI intellectual attribution register — initial insights

**Recorded:** 2026-09-09  
**Purpose:** Preserve the origin, interpretation, and limits of notable project
insights so later contributors can credit source material accurately.

## Attribution method

This register distinguishes three kinds of entries:

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Founder-provided concept | Direction, language, or idea supplied by the founder in source material |
| Collaborative synthesis | A structure or interpretation developed from founder direction in conversation with GitHub Copilot |
| Technical assessment | An implementation, safety, or operations conclusion derived from the repository and deployment state |

Entries are not legal determinations of authorship, ownership, novelty, or
patentability. They record project provenance and should be supplemented by
dated source records, contributor agreements, and qualified legal advice when
formal rights matter.

## Future entry standard

For every material future insight, record:

1. A concise statement of the insight and its practical significance.
2. The source location, recorded date, source hash where available, and the
source contributor or contributor group.
3. Whether it is founder-provided, collaborative synthesis, or technical
assessment.
4. The implementation document, code, or decision it influenced.
5. Any uncertainty, attribution limitation, consent requirement, or legal
review needed.

This approach preserves credit without overstating certainty or turning the
project record into an unsupported legal claim.
