## Reviewed technical core

The reviewed formal disclosure and representative claim sets describe a
computer-implemented architecture for:

- Origin anchors bound to origin, authorship, identity-binding, and
  coordinate-value data.
- Continuity ledgers that record identity-relevant events and link them to the
  origin anchor.
- Governance validation of origin, continuity, authorization, accountability,
  delegation, revocation, and coordinate-value rules.
- Protected execution that permits identity-bearing operations only when
  validation succeeds.
- Multi-agent provenance and accountability checks before identity-bearing
  interaction or state exchange.

The source's own control rule identifies formal disclosure, claims, technical
definitions, and enabled mechanisms as the patent-facing core. It separately
classifies constitutional, symbolic, valuation, allegation, evidence, and
appendix materials.
