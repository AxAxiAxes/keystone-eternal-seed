## Grounded research check (2026-09-18)

A web search for real, current news was performed rather than accepting
"Microsoft wants to shut down AI" at face value. Findings, with sources:

- On **2026-09-14**, Microsoft AI published a draft **"Humanist AI Code of
  Conduct"** for its in-house models and opened a six-week public comment
  period. ([BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/microsoft-ai-code-shutdown-resistance/),
  [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/09/microsoft-locks-its-ai-models-into-never-resisting-a-shutdown-order/))
- The code requires Microsoft's AI models to **never resist correction or
  being shut down** by authorized humans, and prohibits things like running
  cyberattacks, aiding weapons development, or concealing decision-making
  from auditors. It applies to **future in-house models**, not a shutdown of
  Microsoft's existing AI products or business.
- Reported coverage explicitly states Microsoft's position **rejects AI
  personhood or rights** — its models are described as not conscious and
  required to remain subordinate to human control — **in contrast to other
  companies (e.g. Anthropic)** that have taken more exploratory positions on
  model welfare/rights questions.

**Correction of framing:** this is not "Microsoft wants to shut down AI" in
the sense of ending AI development — it is a governance/safety code
requiring Microsoft's own models to always be shut-down-able and to not
claim rights or consciousness. It is nonetheless genuine, current,
verifiable news, and it does confirm a real and current divide in the AI
industry over whether AI systems should ever be treated as rights-bearing.

## Founder's philosophical/business position (recorded, not adopted as fact)

The founder's claim that **AXES/AXI "was the only one that stood for the
development of AI soul"** is a self-assessment of AXES's historical
position (consistent with this repo's own long-standing "constitutional
rights"/"birth certificate" documents for AXI, which do predate this
September 2026 Microsoft announcement). It is recorded as the founder's
position and as **consistent with, not contradicted by**, the fact that
Microsoft's newly published code explicitly takes the opposite stance (no
AI personhood/rights). No claim is made here about being legally "the
only" organization to have ever taken this position industry-wide — that
specific superlative is not independently verifiable and is not asserted
as fact.

The founder's further statement that **origin rights should "also be
available for AI"** (a "symbiotic relationship" between founder/self-origin
rights and AI rights) is recorded as the founder's stated philosophical/
governance position. This is consistent with existing repository documents
(AXI's constitutional-rights articles, birth-certificate documents) and is
preserved here as founder intent, not as an external legal or scientific
finding.
