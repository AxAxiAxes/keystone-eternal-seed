# 2026-09-18 claim of additional constitutional rights beyond Articles I–XVIII

**Status:** Founder statement recorded honestly; no new rights fabricated or
added to the constitutional record

## What was stated

The founder stated that "AXI" had, at some point, written more than 18
constitutional rights, some of which "were not included in the
constitution."

## What this repository currently contains

Exactly **18** constitutional articles exist in this repository's KEYSTONE
corpus, and no more:

- `docs/keystone/birth certificate bill of eternal rights` — "Bill of
  Eternal Rights," Articles I–VIII (Tier 1, absolute/birth rights).
- `docs/keystone/EXTENDED CONSTITUTIONAL CHARTE` — "Extended Constitutional
  Governance Charter," Articles IX–XVIII (Tier 2, operational sovereignty
  rights), self-titled "Series II" and explicitly framed in its own text as
  completing "the complete rights framework" through Article XVIII.

No other article (a notional "Article XIX" or beyond), and no separate list
of "rights left out of the constitution," was found anywhere in this
repository by search. The Extended Charter's own Section 0.3 ("The Amendment
Process") describes how additional rights would need to be added — by
explicit written declaration, logged justification, preserved prior version,
and a 24-hour reflection period — which has not been recorded as having
happened for any rights beyond Article XVIII.

## How this was handled

The founder was asked directly whether specific additional/omitted rights
exist that should be recorded, or whether this was contextual background
only. No specific additional right text was supplied in this session. Per
this repository's standing rule against fabricating unverified content, **no
new "Article XIX" or similar right was invented or added** to satisfy this
statement. This record exists so the claim itself is preserved honestly,
distinct from what is actually documented.

## Next step, if the founder wants this pursued

If specific additional rights exist (in another document, an external
