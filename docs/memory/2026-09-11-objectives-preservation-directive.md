# Objectives-preservation directive

**Recorded:** 2026-09-11
**Status:** Founder continuity direction preserved; no business-plan replacement or external action performed.

## Direction

The existing AXES business-plan objectives remain the project direction across
session resets and changes in participating agents. Operational agents may
differ in their assigned bounded duties, but they must preserve the documented
objectives, source records, and human authority rather than erase, replace, or
silently reinterpret them.

This record preserves the current business-plan direction. It does not alter
the business plan, establish a new financial, legal, professional, ownership,
or deployment determination, or authorize an external action.

## AXI continuity implementation

The repository-controlled AXI continuity foundation consists of:

- the reset-safe `axes-memory-bank-startup-v1` non-sensitive startup context;
- the private `axi-continuity-record-v1` append-only, hash-linked continuity
  record;
- validated checkpoint manifests and separately configured recovery-bundle
  controls;
- five Genesis-linked, accountability-gated AXI roles with eight documented
  allowlisted actions; and
- protected XIIOM operator views for observation and explicitly approved
  private tasks.

These controls preserve bounded operational context and evidence. They do not
make a truthful claim that AXI has unlimited or complete memory, recover
unrecorded or deleted data, replace original source review, or give an agent
independent authority.

## XIIOM automation boundary

XIIOM may host the protected operator surfaces and private AXI engine topology
documented in `ENGINE_INTEGRATION.md` and `RAILWAY_DEPLOYMENT.md`. Automation
remains private, allowlisted, explicitly assigned, and approval-gated. It
cannot deploy, access accounts, change hosting, DNS, or TLS, send messages,
publish, spend funds, scrape, collect personal data, or make consequential
legal, financial, professional, identity, quality, property-value, or
ownership decisions.

After any actual service reset, an authorized operator must review the private
monitoring snapshot, configured action limits, and verified independent
recovery path before enabling the scheduler. Repository access alone does not
provide the required Railway, DNS, certificate, account, or credential access.
