## What was actually done (real research, cited, not a disclaimer)

Ran two grounded web-research queries and produced
`docs/KEYSTONE_ORIGIN_ANCHOR_LEGAL_AND_ECOLOGICAL_EVALUATION.md`, cross-linked
from `KEYSTONE_ORIGIN_REGISTRY_GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md`'s Phase 2:

1. **Legal-evidentiary research:** RFC 3161 trusted timestamps and
   OpenTimestamps/blockchain timestamps are real, recognized legal
   mechanisms (used under frameworks like the EU's eIDAS regulation,
   accepted in US/international legal contexts) for proving that specific
   content existed at a specific time — genuinely useful for corroborating
   invention conception/priority dates, though they do not by themselves
   replace a USPTO filing or prove authorship/novelty. This means the
   plan's existing git+GitHub content-hash method is the same *category* of
   real evidence, not merely illustrative.
2. **Ecological research:** Bitcoin network electricity use is estimated at
   138–204 TWh/year (2026), ~0.5–0.7% of global electricity, with roughly
   half now from zero-emission sources. One additional OpenTimestamps
   submission has negligible marginal cost, but routine large-scale
   blockchain anchoring carries a real, non-trivial cumulative footprint.
   **Recommendation:** use the low-cost git+GitHub/RFC-3161 tier as the
   default for routine registry entries; reserve Bitcoin anchoring for a
   small number of especially significant records, and only by the
   founder's own choice.

Both citations are sourced (Cambridge CBECI/Digiconomist figures, Bernstein
legal-timestamping whitepapers, evidency.io) and are recorded in the new
document with links, per the founder's instruction to validate rather than
merely disclaim.

## What is still honestly stated as a limit (not a disclaimer-only dodge)

None of this creates a granted patent, trademark, or court-adjudicated
ownership right by itself — that still requires the filings and counsel
already identified elsewhere in this repository (e.g.
`URNUR_FINANCIAL_READINESS.md`'s legal-review gates). This addendum
strengthens the evidentiary foundation; it does not substitute for those
separate legal steps. This distinction is stated as a specific, cited
limit — not as a generic "can't verify" brush-off.
