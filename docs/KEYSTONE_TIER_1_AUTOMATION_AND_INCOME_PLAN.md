# KEYSTONE Tier 1 automation and income evaluation

**Status:** Internal zero-spend operating plan  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-10  
**Purpose:** Use already available tools and disciplined internal automation to
reduce administrative overhead, protect source records, and evaluate revenue
without authorizing external account changes or financial activity.

## Scope and conditions

Tier 1 does not assume that Copilot for Outlook, Word, Excel, Teams, or Edge
is enabled, free, licensed, or suitable for the founder's account. The
founder must confirm product availability, tenant/account permissions,
commercial terms, privacy settings, retention, and permitted data use before
putting any private material into an external provider.

The current recurring AXES session automation may maintain this plan's
internal records. It cannot activate Microsoft products, change accounts or
subscriptions, send email, contact clients, publish content, deploy services,
make purchases, alter DNS, submit legal filings, or take cryptocurrency,
banking, payment, trading, custody, or investment action.

## Productivity-tool evaluation protocol

For each already enabled tool, conduct a limited founder-reviewed test using
non-sensitive sample content:

1. Define the job: draft a non-sensitive client follow-up, summarize a
   public-safe note, structure a project checklist, or organize a sample
   spreadsheet.
2. Record the manual baseline time, tool-assisted time, correction time, and
   quality decision.
3. Record the plan/license, account/tenant setting, data-use/retention
   setting, and whether private data was excluded.
4. Keep the tool only if the founder approves output quality and the measured
   net time savings justify its actual cost and privacy tradeoff.

Do not claim 30-50 hours of weekly savings until a measured baseline and
several weeks of actual use support that figure.

## Income evaluation

The earliest revenue is expected from services and products AXES can lawfully
deliver with a clear scope, price, terms, and support path:

| Revenue lane | Near-term evaluation | Decision rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AXES design/materials consultation | Track qualified inquiries, proposals, accepted work, delivery time, direct cost, and client feedback | Continue only when delivery is profitable after founder time, direct costs, and support needs |
| KEYSTONE creator-origin setup | Track interest in private asset inventory, provenance organization, and evidence-packet preparation | Sell only as operational recordkeeping, with clear privacy/confidentiality terms and no legal-advice claim |
| Chichetki instruments/objects | Track product readiness, production cost, fulfillment capacity, and buyer interest | Do not sell until rights, product description, pricing, taxes, returns, and delivery are ready |
| Materials relationships | Track qualified introductions and written supplier terms | Use only transparent disclosed referrals; no deceptive endorsement or unverified product claim |

Income projections must state the offer, price, expected conversion rate,
delivery capacity, direct costs, founder time, and cash-collection assumption.
Use low, base, and high scenarios. Treat a forecast as a decision aid, not
revenue, valuation, or a guarantee.

Use `AXES_TIER_1_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md` for the approved minimum-data template
covering offer readiness, conservative scenario assumptions,
actual-versus-forecast variance, and founder-reviewed productivity tests.
Use `AXES_ASSET_USE_READINESS.md` for the individual asset-use decision fields
that must be complete before any private source asset is reused.
Use `AXES_TIER_1_DECISION_REGISTER.md` to track the human-controlled
activation, continuity, and legal-readiness decisions that remain pending.

## Cryptocurrency and financial boundary

URNUR, Harmonics, and any cryptocurrency, currency, bank, marketplace,
collectible exchange, or trading-app activity may be evaluated only through
internal research and counsel preparation. The Tier 1 automation must not:

- Create wallets, accounts, tokens, smart contracts, keys, exchanges, payment
  links, market-data connections, trading strategies, or transactions.
- Collect customer identity, financial, payment, wallet, or investment data.
- Publish a financial, currency, bank, investment, yield, value, regulatory,
  or availability claim.
- Calculate individualized financial recommendations, rankings, or trading
  signals.

Any later implementation requires the written permitted scope described in
`URNUR_FINANCIAL_READINESS.md`.

## Weekly review

The founder reviews, at minimum:

1. Which safe Tier 1 records or client-readiness tasks were completed.
2. Actual time saved versus the measured baseline.
3. Qualified inquiries, proposals, accepted work, collected revenue, direct
   costs, and uncollected obligations.
4. Whether each tool, feature, or revenue lane should continue, improve,
   pause, or be retired.
5. Any legal, privacy, rights, security, or operational blocker that requires
   human action.
