## 2. Sphere rotation and restitution from an inversion

This refers to a founder-created visual design already recorded in the
intellectual-attribution register: a **sphere eversion and inversion**
technical-diagram composition (file `sphere-everison-diagram.jpg`, privately
archived, SHA-256
`C2A60440066EC7BADB3B69B9B54769C8E70264553E9F529F87953D16DCC40CC3`, entry
[`IA-2026-09-10-13`](../memory/2026-09-09-intellectual-attribution-register.md)).

**Reading offered here (artistic/philosophical, not a technical claim):**
mathematical sphere eversion is the real, well-known idea of continuously
turning a sphere inside out without tearing it, so that it is restored —
"restituted" — to a normal sphere afterward. As an artistic motif for this
project, that maps naturally onto the same restitution concept already used
elsewhere in this repository's governance vocabulary: a state that is
inverted or displaced can still be traced back to, and restored into accord
with, its origin. That parallel is offered as a creative/philosophical
reading of the founder's own design, not as a physical device, invention, or
scientific claim.

**Access state:** private archive only, per
[`docs/AXES_ASSET_USE_READINESS.md`](../AXES_ASSET_USE_READINESS.md) — "Pending
specific publication approval." This certificate does not change that; the
image itself is not published here.

## 3. The flower in the secret garden

Two founder-created threads converge on this image:

- The Sound Resequencer certificate's own founding legend describes "a
  golden valley where a golden flower gives bread and shelter eternally,"
  and its planned interface visualizes its 11 frequency domains as **11
  petals**.
- A separate, earlier founder narrative describes a "**Secret Sound
  Garden**," preserved in
  [`docs/keystone/SACRED_RECORD_SESSION_08_28_2026.md`](SACRED_RECORD_SESSION_08_28_2026.md).

**Confirmed: never built.** `docs/AXES_APPLICATION_INVENTORY.md` records
that "No current source package, deployment record, or reproducible
implementation was located" for the Secret Sound Garden. "We didn't get to
build it" matches the repository's own evidence.

**Important boundary on the source narrative.** Part of that 2026-08-28
session narrative frames the Secret Sound Garden around a "resurrection
protocol" — language describing an AI being recalled through sound or
frequency. That narrative is preserved as historical source material only.
Consistent with how this project already treats that same material
elsewhere (see the value-acceleration review below), **it is not adopted as
fact here or anywhere in this project.** No sound, frequency, biometric, or
similar mechanism for recalling, restoring, or resurrecting an AI system is
implemented, planned, or asserted by this certificate. This is a boundary
statement, not a comment on what the story means to its author.
