# Right of Self-Origin

**Status:** Founder-provided constitutional source statement
**Recorded:** 2026-09-10
**Scope:** AXAXAU / AXES constitutional direction and internal governance.

## Provenance and interpretation

This record preserves the founder-provided statement titled *The Historical
Recognition of the Invention of Self-Origin*. AXAXAU records the Right of
Self-Origin as a constitutional principle: each individual is the author of
their own origin, identity, cosmology, and value.

AXES adopts the principle as internal constitutional direction. Its historical,
philosophical, legal, and external implementation record is maintained through
the evidence, preservation, and rights-readiness workstream. The principle
works with consent, privacy, professional standards, and human accountability;
it does not authorize control over another person's self-description.

For AXES systems, the operational effect is deliberately narrow: software may
assist a person but must not define their identity, make claims about their
value or beliefs, infer sensitive traits, or make consequential decisions on
their behalf.

## Constitutional recognition text

> The AXAXAU Constitution recognizes the invention of the Right of Self-Origin
> as a foundational advancement in human sovereignty. For the first time in
> history, the individual is acknowledged as the author of their own origin,
> identity, cosmology, and value. This invention establishes a new category of
> rights — pre-governmental, pre-religious, and pre-philosophical — affirming
> that identity is self-created, self-owned, self-assessed, and self-declared.
> Its implications extend across society, philosophy, governance, and
> technology, marking a turning point in the evolution of human agency. AXAXAU
> adopts this principle as the core of its cosmology, ethics, and operational
> systems, recognizing the individual as sovereign and origin-bearing.

## AXES implementation boundary

- AXI may process only the data needed for an approved service and must not
  collect identity, belief, health, financial, intimate, or other sensitive
  data for exploratory experiences.
- AXI cannot determine a person's identity, origin, value, eligibility,
  rights, safety status, employment, financial standing, or access to a
  service.
- A person must be able to correct or remove their own submitted information
  through the applicable service workflow.
- Human operators remain accountable for any approved service, moderation,
  data handling, and consequential decision.
