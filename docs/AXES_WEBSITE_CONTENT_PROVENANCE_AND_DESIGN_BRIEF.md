## Founder inputs required before visual concepts

1. Target audiences and the intended first offers, including whether emergency services are in the initial publishing scope.
2. Exact public service descriptions, limits, location/service area, hours/availability, response/contact/escalation workflow, calls to action, and publishing scope.
3. License/certification evidence and publication instructions: holder/entity name, type, issuing authority, jurisdiction, status, number-display decision, expiry/renewal handling, and supported service mapping.
4. Confirmed legal/display-name treatment for headers, footer, legal notices, and wordmark use.
5. Approved visual references, original/cleared assets, logo, colors, typography, and an item-level rights/credit/accessibility record.
6. Approved public contact route, privacy notice/path, data collection/retention/consent design, content owner, and correction/removal path.
7. Chosen public domain/host, page architecture, noindex/indexing decision, analytics/integration decision, and named founder approver.

## Deliberate website-design process

1. **Discovery:** confirm the founder inputs above; create a claim-and-asset evidence register; decide publication scope.
2. **Concepts:** develop two or three original, rights-cleared visual directions using the cited themes without representing unverified services or credentials.
3. **Selection:** founder selects a concept, legal/display-name treatment, approved content boundary, and accessibility direction.
4. **Page architecture:** map approved pages, information hierarchy, emergency/non-emergency contact paths, disclaimers, privacy path, and claim-to-evidence ownership.
5. **Visual design:** produce responsive designs and content specifications; record every asset's source, rights, use, credit, and accessibility treatment.
6. **Implementation:** build only the approved architecture/content; retain noindex and omit structured data, forms, third-party scripts, and claims until their gates are met.
7. **Review:** founder and appropriate qualified reviewers validate exact claims, credential/emergency presentation, accessibility, privacy, links, and a private preview against the evidence register.
8. **Deployment:** deploy only after explicit founder authorization; verify the intended route and controls; authorize indexing separately and maintain a correction/removal process.
