# AXES design and materials consultation readiness

**Status:** Internal candidate-offer sheet; not approved for publication,
solicitation, pricing, or paid delivery
**Recorded:** 2026-09-10
**Accountable owner:** Founder approval required before activation
**Purpose:** Define a bounded, non-professional AXES consultation candidate so
the founder can decide whether it is ready for a small lawful pilot.

## Candidate offer

**Working identifier:** `AXES-DMC-001`
**Audience:** People seeking aesthetic direction for a personal space or
project before engaging qualified construction, design, engineering, inspection,
appraisal, real-estate, or other professionals.

**Supported outcome:** A founder-reviewed visual/material direction brief that
organizes a client-provided objective, inspiration, palette, finishes, and
questions to take to their own qualified providers.

**Candidate deliverables:**

1. One written visual-direction brief based only on client-provided,
   non-sensitive project information and appropriately sourced references.
2. One material/palette direction board using founder-created, licensed,
   permission-based, or public manufacturer material approved for the use.
3. A list of general discussion questions for the client to take to qualified
   providers.
4. One bounded correction pass limited to fidelity with the agreed aesthetic
   brief.

**Candidate delivery boundary:** Delivery is a private founder-controlled
document through an approved channel. It is not a construction drawing,
specification, estimate, inspection report, site assessment, appraisal, code
analysis, permit document, procurement instruction, or professional opinion.

## Explicit exclusions

AXES must not present or provide this candidate offer as:

- licensed architecture, interior design where licensed scope applies,
  engineering, construction management, code compliance, safety analysis,
  inspection, or appraisal;
- a property valuation, real-estate recommendation, brokerage/referral,
  contractor selection, background check, license verification, or guarantee;
- product availability, pricing, compatibility, performance, installation,
  suitability, warranty, tax, or purchasing advice;
- a payment, marketplace, order, fulfillment, or transaction service; or
- legal, financial, insurance, accessibility certification, or medical advice.

The client remains responsible for engaging and relying on appropriately
qualified professionals for all regulated, technical, construction, safety,
code, property, financial, and legal decisions.
