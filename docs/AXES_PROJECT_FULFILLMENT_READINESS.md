# AXES project fulfillment and transaction-progress readiness

**Status:** Future private workspace; no transaction-monitoring service is active  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-10  
**Scope:** Private, project-specific support for client/provider agreements,
delivery milestones, price-list versions, satisfaction feedback, and
human-reviewed discrepancy handling.

## Purpose

AXES may provide a private project workspace so a client and provider can
understand a defined service from agreed scope through delivery. The workspace
may organize project documents, scope versions, price-list configurations,
milestones, change requests, delivery evidence, and private satisfaction
feedback.

The longer-term product is a curated service marketplace with understandable
price-list information and a client-to-provider matching path. It may help a
client find an approved category of service and request an introduction to a
participating provider. Marketplace matching is informational and
human-reviewed; it is not an automated recommendation, professional referral,
ranking, endorsement, guarantee of availability, license verification, safety
assessment, quality certification, or promise of a particular outcome.

Terms such as **transaction-progress resequencer** and **price-list
configuration resequencer** mean versioned organization of a project's
approved scope, pricing assumptions, milestones, and changes. They do not
create a price, negotiate value, modify an agreement, determine satisfaction,
or bind either party.

## First-pilot boundaries

- Provider profiles, service categories, and price-list information must be
  owner-submitted or explicitly confirmed, dated, and correctable.
- A match request may be routed only with the participant's permission and a
  stated purpose. No scraping, unsolicited outreach, personal-data brokerage,
  or lead resale.
- Feedback is private, project-specific, voluntary, and visible only to the
  participating client, provider, and named AXES review owner.
- No public provider ratings, rankings, reputation scores, review marketplace,
  lead resale, or profile-based recommendation system.
- Payments, deposits, escrow, payment routing, release of funds, lending,
  currency conversion, and collections occur outside AXES/URNUR.
- The workspace does not act as a broker, appraiser, inspector, engineer,
  attorney, insurer, guarantor, arbitrator, or financial institution.
- No automated negotiation, price setting, contract acceptance, fraud finding,
  eligibility decision, dispute decision, or consequential action.
- Do not collect identity documents, payment details, bank information, wallet
  addresses, credit information, or unnecessary personal information.

## Private project record

For each approved project, maintain only the information needed to support the
stated workflow:

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Parties and roles | Identify the participating client/provider and named human review owner |
| Scope and agreement reference | Record the version the parties say they agreed to; do not replace the signed agreement |
| Price-list configuration | Preserve the approved price/estimate version, currency, assumptions, exclusions, effective date, and change history |
| Milestones | Record planned and completed deliverables, dates, acceptance notes, and supporting evidence |
| Change requests | Preserve requester, requested change, revised scope/price reference, status, and human approvals |
| Delivery evidence | Link approved documents, photographs, messages, or acceptance records with access limits |
| Private feedback | Optional factual delivery/satisfaction feedback, correction route, visibility, and review status |
| Discrepancy record | Record a reported mismatch or concern, evidence, interim action, and human follow-up; not a fraud determination |

## Human review and discrepancy handling

The workspace may flag incomplete milestones, conflicting versions, missing
delivery evidence, or participant-reported concerns. A flag is a request for
human review, not a fraud conclusion. The named owner must:

1. Preserve the relevant project record without altering originals.
2. Notify only the authorized participants through an approved channel.
3. Allow factual correction and response.
4. Document the decision, next step, and whether the project remains active,
   paused, or referred to a qualified professional.
5. Avoid public accusations or sharing of private records.

## Readiness gate

Before a private pilot, define the actual service category and jurisdiction;
named human support/review owner; participant terms; consent and confidentiality
notice; data map; role-based access; retention, export, and deletion process;
security review; accessibility review; incident/escalation route; and
appropriate California professional, consumer-protection, privacy, and
contract review.

Before a public marketplace or matching pilot, also define provider
participation terms, listing data fields, price-list update/expiration rules,
match-request consent, correction/removal process, disclosure language,
provider-category boundaries, unfair/deceptive-practice controls, accessibility,
and a human owner for listing and match review.
