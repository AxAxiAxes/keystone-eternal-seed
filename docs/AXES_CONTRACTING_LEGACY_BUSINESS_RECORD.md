**Status:** Historical record only, sourced from public Internet Archive
Wayback Machine snapshots. Not a current service claim, not a licensing or
insurance status claim, and not itself authorization to publish these
service claims on any live AXES Contracting page.
**Recorded:** 2026-09-18/19, following the founder's note that
`axescontracting.com` "used to be on GoDaddy for like 10 years" and a
suggestion to check web archives.

## What was found

`axescontracting.com` had an active, GoDaddy Website Builder–hosted public
site (`meta name="generator" content="Starfield Technologies; Go Daddy
Website Builder 7.0.5350"`) with essentially unchanged content from at
least **2018 through 2025** — the Wayback Machine holds snapshots from

captures (most recent found: 2025-11-10), all showing the same four-page

## What this means for AXES Contracting today

This describes a **real, decade-plus operating business**: property/home
inspection, environmental hazard identification (fire, moisture, chemical,
biological), damage remediation, and insurance-accepted reporting — plus
general residential/commercial contracting and remodeling. This is
materially different from, and likely predates, the "design/materials
consultation" framing currently documented as AXES Contracting's initial
paid offering in `docs/AXES_BUSINESS_PLAN.md` and
`docs/AXES_DESIGN_MATERIALS_CONSULTATION_READINESS.md`.

**This is a significant, unresolved open item, not yet reconciled:**

- If this service line (home inspection, hazard remediation, insurance
  reporting) is **still actively licensed, insured, and operating** today,
  it is a far more revenue-ready offering than a from-scratch design
  consultation product — it has ten-plus years of real client history.
- Home inspection and hazard-remediation work is **licensed/regulated
  professional activity** in California (contractor licensing, and in some
  cases specialized certifications for mold/asbestos/lead work). This
  repository has no visibility into current license status, insurance
  coverage, or active operating capacity, and per this project's standing
  qualification/authority boundary, **must not** assert or imply active
  licensing, certification, insurance-acceptance, or "certified home
  inspector" claims on any public page without explicit founder
  confirmation that those credentials are current.
- **No changes were made to any live public page based on this archive.**
  This record exists so the founder can decide, with real information,
  whether to (a) confirm the legacy service line is still active and have
  it become the featured public offering (potentially superseding or
  supplementing the design-consultation plan), (b) present it purely as
  company history/credibility ("10+ years serving Southern California
  property owners") without claiming current active licensing, or (c)
  leave the current design-focused plan as the sole public claim and treat
  this only as background history.

## Recommended founder decision (added to `FOUNDER_ACTION_QUEUE.md` item 12)

Confirm current status of the home-inspection/hazard-remediation service

inspection/remediation service claims until this is confirmed.
