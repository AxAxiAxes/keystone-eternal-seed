# AXES Tier 1 evidence ledger

**Status:** Internal measurement template; no offer price, client activity, or
productivity savings is approved or recorded here
**Recorded:** 2026-09-10
**Owner:** Founder or named human delegate
**Purpose:** Provide a repeatable, minimum-data structure for evaluating safe
AXES and KEYSTONE early-revenue work before it is offered, published, or
expanded.

## Operating boundary

This ledger is a template, not a sales plan, invoice system, client database,
pricing authorization, financial statement, or productivity claim. It must not
contain client names, contact information, project addresses, contracts,
payment information, account credentials, private archive contents, legal
advice, or source evidence. Keep detailed private records in a founder-approved
private location.

Only evaluate work that is permitted by
`FOUNDER_REVENUE_PRIORITY_OVERLAY.md` and
`KEYSTONE_TIER_1_AUTOMATION_AND_INCOME_PLAN.md`:

- AXES non-professional design/materials consultation, visual concept work, and
  project documentation.
- KEYSTONE private asset inventory, provenance organization, and lawyer-ready
  evidence-packet preparation as operational recordkeeping, not legal advice.

Do not use this ledger to evaluate cryptocurrency, URNUR, Harmonics, banking,
trading, payments, marketplaces, collectible exchange, valuation, appraisal,
inspection, engineering, architecture, brokerage, or other regulated work.

## Offer-readiness record

Complete one record for each candidate offer before accepting paid work. A
blank or unapproved field means the offer is not ready.

| Field | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Offer identifier and date | Internal identifier; record date |
| Customer and problem | General audience and supported need; no client personal data |
| Permitted scope | Concrete deliverables, limits, expected turnaround, and support path |
| Exclusions | Professional, legal, financial, payment, and other excluded activities |
| Accountable owner | Named human responsible for delivery and support |
| Price assumption | Proposed fixed/range/hourly basis, included work, direct costs, and tax/expense treatment; founder approval required before use |
| Capacity assumption | Founder hours per delivery and maximum concurrent work |
| Rights and privacy | Asset source class, approved use, confidentiality treatment, and any needed review |
| Terms readiness | Required client terms, cancellation/change process, and record location |
| Approval decision | Founder approver, date, decision, and required follow-up |

## Review and escalation

The founder reviews entries at least weekly while an offer is under
consideration. Pause the offer and escalate for human review if any required
scope, rights, privacy, support, professional, tax, consumer-protection, or
legal requirement is unresolved. Do not use an incomplete record to accept
work, publish a price, contact a client, process payment, or make a public
performance claim.
