## 3. Synopsis: how this work could benefit the public

Kept strictly to what is real and buildable today:

- **AXIOM chat** (`xiiom.com/axiom`) is a real, live, OpenAI-backed product
  with a working identity/date system prompt and persistent memory (this
  session's and PR #73/#95's merged work) — a genuinely useful, no-cost
  public-facing conversational tool today, independent of any future
  monetization.
- **AXES-DMC-001**, once activated, would offer people a low-cost,
  non-professional first pass at visual/material direction for a personal
  project before they engage (and pay for) licensed professionals — reducing
  wasted time and cost for people who are not yet ready to hire an architect,
  designer, or contractor.
- **KEYSTONE's provenance/evidence-organization tooling** (creator-origin
  record setup, private asset inventory) could help ordinary creators
  organize their own authorship records before a dispute arises — a real,
  lawful, non-legal-advice service already scoped in
  `KEYSTONE_TIER_1_AUTOMATION_AND_INCOME_PLAN.md`.
